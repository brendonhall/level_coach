## 3. Rotation and playstyle by level band

**1–9** — Opener: Immolate to engage, then Shadow Bolt and auto-attacks to finish (method); from 4 Immolate + Corruption, then auto/Shadow Bolt (method). tavern-leveling early rotation: send Imp/Voidwalker in → Bane of Agony (from 8) to pull, it ramps slowly so the pet gets threat → Corruption (from 4) → Immolate on anything that will live long enough → wand until dead (Shadow Bolt and/or main-hand before you own a wand). Shadow Bolt is very expensive at 1–5, expect to wait for mana (method). Life Tap from 6: tap between pulls, carry food to eat the health back (method, tavern-leveling); at 6 one tap is roughly a third of your health bar (tavern-class). Second mob: Fear it, DoT it, finish the first (tavern-leveling). Pet: Imp out at all times for damage (method); grab Grimoire of Blood Pact at 4 (method). Apply Demon Skin and keep it up (method). Get a wand as early as possible (method, tavern-leveling, tavern-class). wowforever.gg priority (all levels): let the demon secure threat, then Corruption and a curse; fill with Shadow Bolt or a drain; use Nightfall procs immediately; Life Tap only with a safe health reserve, then recover that health.

**10–19** — Voidwalker from 10 tanks; always send the pet in (pet attack) before you cast (method). Affliction opener: Corruption + Bane of Agony, then wand (or Drain Life only if you need the health) (method). tavern-leveling rotation: send demon → Bane of Agony to pull → Corruption → Immolate on long-lived mobs → wand until dead → Life Tap whenever you have more health than mana. Drain Life (14) is the drain-tank tool but wanding is the filler until 29 (method, tavern-leveling). Wand DPS is the top early stat (tavern-leveling). Drain Soul (10) rank 1 for shards while the pet tanks (tavern-leveling). Multi-mob: DoT the first and Fear it, kill the second with the pet, re-Fear as needed (tavern-leveling). Pull with Bane of Agony because it instant-tags and its first ticks are small; avoid opening with Immolate's upfront hit when the pet needs threat (tavern-leveling).

**20–29** — Sayaad (Succubus/Incubus) from 20 becomes the pet most builds level with: it deals the most damage of any demon but holds threat worse than the Voidwalker, so let it hit the mob for a second before your first big spell (tavern-leveling). method: drain tanking becomes possible at 20 but is not recommended before 30 (better Drain Life ranks and Soul Siphon). tavern-leveling: keep wanding until 29; take Improved Drains at 27–30 "ready for drain tanking at 30"; if your wand still out-damages Drain Life, keep wanding and take Improved Drains only when you switch. tavern-class: fill with the wand, not Shadow Bolt, once DoTs are up; level with the Sayaad once you can handle aggro yourself. Curse of the Elements (20) is a utility Curse that stacks with Bane of Agony (tavern-leveling, tavern-class). Fear a second mob rather than losing it; Curse of Recklessness cancels a Fear that runs the wrong way (tavern-leveling).

**30–39** — Drain tanking (method): keep Corruption + Bane of Agony on the mobs, Succubus out, let the mobs hit you, Drain Life to heal while damaging, Life Tap for mana, Drain Life again to recover the tapped health. Why: almost no eating/drinking, few stops, more damage from the Succubus than the Voidwalker, only food needed in the bags (method). tavern-leveling rotation from 30: send demon → Bane of Agony → Corruption → Immolate on long-lived mobs → Drain Life until dead; if at full health, Life Tap first; switch to the wand only when the mob is almost dead; Life Tap whenever health exceeds mana. Fight two at a time (DoT + Fear the first, pet on the second; avoid Shadow Bolt and Immolate's initial hit on the Feared mob since a big hit can break Fear); with enough healing take a third and tank it yourself with Drain Life (tavern-leveling). Health and mana are one pool: cast Life Tap when health far exceeds mana (don't sit at full health while Drain Life is healing you), cast Drain Life when mana far exceeds health, never wand at full mana; with Improved Life Tap each drained health point is worth 1.2 mana (tavern-leveling). Spellstone (36) as your weapon oil (tavern-leveling). Felhunter (30) for caster-heavy or PvP content (tavern-leveling).

**40–49** — Open with Curse of the Elements (rank 3 at 40, +8% Magic damage taken) on high-health mobs, then Bane of Agony, Corruption, Immolate and Siphon Life on anything that lives long enough, Drain Life filler (tavern-leveling). Felsteed (40) speeds travel; Howl of Terror (40) for overpulls; Death Coil (42) as an instant escape that heals (tavern-leveling). method moves into Demonology at 46 (Unholy Power to buff the Succubus, then Demonic Energies, Fel Vitality); timesaver takes Siphon Life at 40, Malevolence 41–45, Improved Bane of Agony 46–47, Nightfall 48–49; wowforever.gg has Wrack from 40 as a core talent and finishes Demonic Embrace + Unholy Power by 50. Dual Talent Specialization at 40 for a dungeon/PvP build (tavern-leveling).

**50–60** — Same drain-tank loop. Inferno (50) and Ritual of Doom (60) cost reagents and are risky, not needed while leveling (tavern-leveling). Bane of Doom (60) only on targets that live past 1 minute (tavern-leveling, tavern-class). Dreadsteed is a long chain at 60 (tavern-leveling). method's last five points (56–60): Improved Bane of Agony, Grim Reach, Suppression 5/5. At 60 raid level, Wrack is the best single-target filler and a Shadow Bolt filler build is close behind (tavern-class).

**Pet role by band (all sources)**: Imp 1–10 (ranged damage, Blood Pact), Voidwalker 10–20 (tank; Sacrifice shield for emergencies; safest for multiple or hard-hitting mobs), Sayaad 20+ (highest damage; the drain-tank partner), Felhunter for casters/PvP (Spell Lock, Devour Magic), Voidwalker again whenever you keep taking hits (tavern-leveling Destruction note). Keep pet Attack and Follow on the scroll wheel or mouse side buttons; the new Move command places the demon anywhere (tavern-leveling).

## 6. Gear, stats, professions, First Aid, macros

**Stat priority.** tavern-leveling early (1–9): Wand DPS > Spell Power/Intellect > Stamina/Spirit. Affliction leveling: Spell Power/Shadow Damage > Wand DPS (until 30) > Spirit/Intellect > Stamina. tavern-class level-60 raid Affliction: Spell Power/Shadow Damage > Crit > Hit; Haste is worth less (it only speeds Shadow Bolt and the GCD). method: prioritise +spell damage while leveling. **Weapons.** Main hand is a stat stick — its Spell Power matters, not its damage (tavern-leveling). method prefers staves unless a one-hander + off-hand gives more spell damage; Staff of Jordan is BiS to 60 but a pricey world BoE. method staff list: Makeshift Stormcaller (Stormy Potential, Horde, 7); Apprentice's Spellstaff (Rare Books, Alliance, 7); Darkwood Staff (The Family Crypt, Horde, 13); Oakthrush Staff (The Fragments Within, Alliance, 13); Staff of Orgrimmar (Hidden Enemies, Ragefire Chasm quest, Horde, 16); Emberstone Staff (Captain Greenskin, Deadmines, 17); Rod of the Sleepwalker (Twilight Lord Kelris, BFD, 24); Hydrocane (Vicious Fallout, Gnomeregan, 27); Spine of the Devourer (Mana Devourer, City of Dalaran, 28); Illusionary Rod (Arcanist Doan, Scarlet Monastery, 34); Greatstaff of the Necrokhans (Khan Jehn, Desolace, 38); Zum'rah's Vexing Cane (Witch Doctor Zum'rah, Zul'Farrak, 43). tavern-leveling: Staff of the Shade (Razorfen Kraul, +37 Shadow Damage, 15 Stamina, very rare, excellent from 22); the Soran'ruk staff/orb at 20; Enchanters craft caster staves and off-hands only they can use; from 30 take quest/dungeon weapons with Spell Power or Shadow/Fire damage. **Professions.** method: Tailoring + Enchanting (disenchant what you craft; Enchanting makes staves early, no BoP wands early). tavern-leveling: Cooking (Well Fed +5% kill XP, needed for your own campsite), First Aid, Tailoring (camp buff gives Spirit for Life Tap), Engineering (camp workbench sells reagents and later repairs; with Tailoring the most damage at 60), Enchanting (Lesser/Greater Magic Wands, staves, oils), Herbalism (gold plus an Intellect camp buff), Alchemy (consumables plus a mana-regen buff), Skinning (income plus a 2% Crit camp buff). tavern-class: Engineering + Tailoring is highest damage, Enchanting + Tailoring the easier pair; Tailoring also makes a Faction Banner camp object (Spirit); Enchanters can now make Staves and Off-Hands.

**Macros / keybinds.** No source gives macro text. tavern-leveling: set the second mob as focus and use a modifier (e.g. Shift) to cast DoTs on focus; bind pet Attack and Follow to the scroll wheel or mouse side buttons; use the new Move command to position the pet. method: always pet-attack before casting.

## 7. Forever changes versus Classic that matter for leveling

**Banes versus Curses.** Curses are split into damage Banes and utility Curses; one Bane and one Curse of yours can sit on a target (tavern-leveling, tavern-class, wft, foreverchanges). Bane of Agony / Bane of Doom are the renamed Curse of Agony / Curse of Doom (all). Numbers: Bane of Agony R6 552 (Classic 1044) but 13.3% SP coefficient (Classic 8.3%); Bane of Doom 1742 (Classic 3200) but 400% coefficient (Classic 100%) (foreverchanges). Curse of Shadow removed; Curse of the Elements now covers every magic school, ranks 1–3 come earlier and a new rank 4 exists (tavern-class, foreverchanges). Curse of Recklessness: −armor only (175 at R1), no damage buff to the mob, still stops fleeing/ignores Fear (tavern-class, foreverchanges). Curse of Exhaustion (talent): 30% slow (Classic 10%), moved tier 5→4; Improved Curse of Exhaustion removed (foreverchanges). ## 8. Undead race notes

- **Will of the Forsaken** — breaks or prevents Charm, Fear and Sleep; the strongest Warlock PvP racial, like a free PvP trinket (tavern-class); great for breaking Charms, Fears and Sleeps in PvP or PvE (method).

## 9. Caveats

**Dating and data vintage**
- method: "Last Updated: 8th November 2026", yet the closing paragraph says the guide will be extended to cover 1–60 after full release, so the leveling detail is pre-release/early-launch. Talent builds are embedded images; only the narrative text was captured, so per-level timing (other than level 46) is derived here, and the race tier list is unreadable.
- tavern-leveling: no explicit date in the body; sidebar shows blue posts from 6 November 2026 and "Class Tuning Incoming - 10 November", so numbers may shift. Talent lists are "in about this order" within bands; the Siphon Life timing is internally inconsistent (rotation says from 41, sequence implies 51).
- tavern-class: "Last updated November 3, 2026"; notes Wrack "now deals far more damage than in the beta", i.e. tuning moved between beta and this text.
- timesaver: build is second-hand from Maxroll (updated 2026-11-03), "checked against the live talent trees on 6 November 2026"; trees come from client data including the 2 November development notes.
- wowforever.gg: "Updated: 3 November 2026"; talent data is a third-party dataset (talentsforever.com, CC BY 4.0, "adapted"); only decade snapshots, no rationale beyond three priority bullets.
- wft: explicitly "FOREVER BETA DATA" from the 4 November 2026 beta spellbook, client 1.60.1.70755; says tooltips are not final, "ranks can still change before launch", "This site has not checked these ranks in-game", "Not Blizzard-verified".
- foreverchanges: build 1.60.1.70862 with the 6 November hotfix, updated 7 November; status bar reads "Realms offline"; its version picker lists builds from 24 September to 7 November, so values are moving. Some entries are data artifacts: Demonic Brand's tooltip reads "1 to 1 Fire or Shadow damage", and the Drain Mana Classic baseline shows "50000 yd, Instant". Only rank-1 tooltips are shown in the text capture.

**Unconfirmed or conflicting items**
- Pandemic's tree tier is not stated anywhere; method's point order requires it to be reachable with 10 Affliction points.
- Life Tap base conversion: foreverchanges 60 health → 60 mana at rank 1 vs tavern-class "20 + Spirit".
- Shadowburn (tavern 20 vs method 24) and Conflagrate (tavern 25 vs method 29) differ only because method's Destruction build spends 4 points in Suppression first.
- Gravestone Scepter: method lists level 22; tavern says the quest opens at 18 but you'll need to be higher.
- Voidwalker quest start: tavern names Razor Hill / Brill (and Goldshire / Ironforge); method speaks of the Orgrimmar / Undercity trainers.
- Suppression: method says 4 points means you never miss same-level mobs; tooltips give +1% hit per rank (timesaver, foreverchanges) — the page does not state the base miss chance behind that claim.
- Portal of Summoning appears only in foreverchanges; the two guide sites still call Ritual of Summoning the only summon.
- Felsteed/Dreadsteed tooltips dropped their speed text (foreverchanges) — a tooltip change, not necessarily a speed change.
- tavern-class says Incinerate "takes 30 points in Destruction" while tavern-leveling places it at level 40 (31 points).
- method's Key Spells table lists Siphon Life as a level-30 trainer spell; it is a talent.
- The Legacy "Talented" perk can move every talent level in section 2 up to 5 levels earlier (tavern-leveling).

**Commercial bias**
- timesaver.gg is a gold-selling and powerleveling vendor (Firenova OÜ, Tallinn): the page carries repeated "Buy WoW Forever gold" and "powerleveling" calls to action and a disclaimer that using them can breach the publisher's terms. Its build content is copied from Maxroll, not original.
- Warcraft Tavern pages carry a Zygor Leveling Guides affiliate link ("we may earn a small commission").
- Method's page is ad-supported (adblock notices, Method Premium "Remove Ads", a VPN discount banner); the author is a Retail Affliction main who streams.
- wowforever.gg, wowforevertalent.com and foreverchanges.pro show no ads or sales content; all three are unofficial fan projects.
