## 1. Forever systems that affect leveling

### Level cap and XP
- Level cap is 60 and will not be raised; leveling happens across the Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor, with new and expanded zones, quests and character options layered on the original. [blizzard]
- Cap of 60 "indefinitely"; design pillars include no level-cap increases (you catch up only by leveling), no level scaling, no flying mounts. [wiki]
- Beta XP tuning: from 1 October dungeon quests give half the bonus XP they used to; from 8 October dungeon kills give about 20% more XP. The page's advice: run each dungeon once, at the right level, with its quests in hand. [wfl-horde]
- Launch respawns: starting areas have the fastest temporary respawn rates; rates slow as players spread out and lower-level zones normalize later. No exact timers published (Blizzard 2 November dev notes, post 9). [wfguides-start]
- Beta note: Loch Modan's "Snowbound" gave more XP than intended; fix slated for a later beta build. [wfguides-start]
- Cozy Sleeping Bag (carried over from Season of Discovery): stacking XP buff plus a portable rest area that grants rested XP while logged out. Scavenger hunt starts at level 14 in Westfall or the Barrens with the quest [40] ...and that note you found; about an hour of travel through level 25–30 areas; also rewards a 12-slot Sturdy Lunchbox and 8 Student Fodder (each restores 500 HP, 900 mana, 50 rage, 100 energy and 176 HP every 2 sec for 12 sec). [wiki]
- Same bag per wfl-horde: up to +3% XP; Horde start the chain at the Burned-Out Remains in the southern Barrens (46, 74) (per Warcraft Tavern); the chain visits Westfall, Stonetalon, Loch Modan and Hillsbrad; the Loch Modan leg means passing level 30 Alliance guards, so do it when you are already in the Eastern Kingdoms. Flagged (7 November) as third-party reporting, not confirmed Forever mechanics; Forever guides disagree on the buff duration (1 hour vs 2 hours); the officially announced Camp Tent is a different system. [wfl-horde]
- Chronoboon Displacer exists, but world buffs are stripped inside dungeons and raids, so it only matters in the open world. [wiki]
- Teaser only (sidebar headline of an 8 November article): quests at level 60 no longer pay bonus gold in place of XP, attributed to JokerdTV on stream 7 November. [timesaver-mount]

### Talents and dual spec
- Classes are "revisited" with talent and ability updates rooted in original WoW; a familiar class name is not a guarantee of the modern build. Hunters can use traps in combat; pets scale with gear and have unique family abilities. [blizzard]
- Talent trees and specs rebalanced with the intent that each class is viable; all nine classes have new or updated abilities and talents. [wiki]
- Talents start at level 10; many Horde classes get their key leveling tool around then (Hunter pet, Warlock Voidwalker, Shaman totems). [wfl-horde]
- Dual talent specialization: available at level 40 from class trainers for 50 (the currency icon was stripped from the page text; presumably gold). Swap freely between two specs outside combat and battlegrounds. The respec fee still applies when changing talents within the active spec; each spec has its own fee that slowly decays to 1 over time. [wiki]
- Level 40 brings riding and dual specialization. [wfl-horde]
- Sidebar teaser headline: "Dual Spec and Respec Cost: Level 40 Unlock, Resets From 1g" (8 November). [timesaver-mount]

### Legacy System
- Account-wide progression through challenges, Legacy Points, character-specific perks and cosmetic rewards. Points are spent per character across three trees: Professions, Adventure and Resourcefulness. At launch each character can spend up to 16 Legacy Points; the cap is reachable on one character, alts are not required. [blizzard]
- Forever's version of achievements. Legacy Challenges such as leveling a class to 25, 40 and 60 award Legacy Points. Points are earned account-wide; each character fills in and respecs its own trees at class trainers. Perks named: [Well Rested] and [Talented] (leveling help), [Working Overtime] (+4–20% chance to gain a tradeskill point), [Bountiful Harvest] (gathering), [Dedicated Study] (daily cooldown; raises your lowest learned tradeskill by one point). 65 Legacy Points exist in total at launch. Account-wide rewards at thresholds: 15 → [Holstered Replica Ironforge Air Rifle] (toy); 25 → [Spectral Bear Cub] (companion); 40 → [Spectral Bear Tabard]; 55 → [Reins of the Spectral Bear] (mount). Leveling the six primary non-gathering tradeskills also awards Legacy Points. [wiki]
- Note: "16 per character at launch" [blizzard] and "65 total at launch" [wiki] measure different things (per-character spend cap vs account-wide pool) — not a contradiction, but keep both numbers.
- Level 25: your first Legacy Point. [wfl-horde]
- Bartering Legacy talent: up to 10% off mount purchases, not off riding training. [method-mounts] Repeated. [timesaver-mount]
- Legacy Points are shared account-wide and spent per character; the Professions tree has Bountiful Harvest for more gathered materials. [timesaver-mount]

### Mounts and riding (details and gold in section 5)
- First mount at level 40 (+60% speed); Epic mount at 60 (+100%), priced so that fresh 60s often cannot afford it. [method-mounts] Same levels and speeds. [timesaver-mount, wfl-mounts]
- Blizzard confirmed a Burning Crusade-style model in its Deep Dive recap: the up-front mount cost shifts into training and a mount is granted when you learn the riding skill. [timesaver-mount, wfl-mounts]
- Apprentice Riding at 40 = 100 gold, bought from your own race's Riding Instructor, first mount (one color of your racial mount) included; extra colors 20 gold each. [method-mounts] 100 gold at 40 with mount included, from the beta client. [timesaver-mount] "About 100 g". [wfl-mounts] "Reported at 100 gold including the mount" (beta players). [wfl-horde]
- Journeyman Riding at 60 = 1,000 gold; Epic Swift mounts 200 gold each. [method-mounts] 1,000 gold for the skill only; no source says it grants a free epic mount; epic mounts about 200 gold (citing Method). [timesaver-mount, wfl-mounts]
- You cannot ride other races' mounts until Exalted with that race; you must travel back to your own racial vendor. [method-mounts, timesaver-mount, wfl-mounts]
- Store mounts (Cerulean Prideclaw; Veteran Adventurer's Loyal Companion) still require buying the riding skill; beta datamines list the Prideclaw as a level 60 mount, so use at 40 is unknown. [method-mounts, timesaver-mount, wfl-mounts] Both are listed as ground mounts in the Skyborne packs. [wiki]
- Class mounts: see section 5 (Paladin/Warlock, including Forsaken Paladin specifics and the unresolved "does the quest also teach riding" question).
- Riding levels were kept at 40/60 despite a long beta forum thread titled "Riding needs to be at level 20 not level 40". [timesaver-mount]
- No flying. [wiki, timesaver-mount, wfl-mounts]

### Professions / tradeskills
- Two primary tradeskills plus all three secondaries (Cooking, First Aid, Fishing). Each tradeskill has dozens of new or updated recipes, many from the Azeroth Commerce Authority or Durotar Supply and Logistics (both returning from Season of Discovery). High-end recipes cost [Merchant's Favor] and have a reputation requirement. Loot crates found in the world are filled and turned in at a faction camp for reputation and Merchant's Favor: Azeroth Commerce Authority at [10, 73], Three Corners, Redridge Mountains; Durotar Supply and Logistics at [42, 34], west of the Crossroads in the Barrens. Notable changes: basic healing potions (e.g. [Minor Healing Potion]) moved from Alchemy to First Aid; armor crafters have many new Bind-on-Pickup patterns; the tauren racial [Cultivation] duplicates herb nodes. [wiki]
- Starter gathering quest around level 3 or 4 rewards a choice of three manuals (Herbalism, Mining or Skinning); a manual only teaches its profession if you do not already have it and hold fewer than two professions (Wowhead, 18 September). Warcraft Tavern names the rewards Wild Harvest, Mining for Dummies and Pelt Collecting for Beginners, each granting 2 skill points. In the Valley of Trials the giver is Galgar and the quest asks for 10 cactus apples. Each starting valley has an Apprentice-only Herbalism trainer and an Apprentice-only Mining trainer. 1 October beta: fewer Poor Copper Vein nodes in starter areas. [wfguides-start]
- Gathering professions recommended from level 1 to fund riding on an empty economy. [timesaver-mount, wfl-mounts]
- Merchant's Favor is sold as a service (from $5.69 per 100) — commercial. [timesaver-mount]

### Camping and rest
- Campsites and profession features bring players together; sitting briefly by a campfire to become rested lets you receive campsite buffs before moving on; the world's dangers are not paused while you rest. [blizzard]
- Camping builds on the Cooking Fire. Unlock at roughly level 5 by finding an adventurer outside your starting zone's major quest hub and completing a few quests such as [6] The Great Outdoors. All tradeskills provide upgradable camping utility from skill level 20. Camping buffs do not stack with the equivalent class buff (e.g. the First Aid [First Aid Kit] stamina buff vs a priest's [Power Word: Fortitude]). High-end camping abilities drop in dungeons and raids and can provide crafting stations required for high-end crafting. [wiki]
- Beta tuning (1 October): campfires deal no damage to players below level 5 and reduced damage to players below 90% health. (24 September): you can craft, cast and buff while waiting for a campfire buff; sitting, kneeling or lying down all count. [wfguides-start]
- Headline teaser only: "Pouch of Violet Dust ... Double Your Campsite Benefits". [method-mounts]

### Itemization
- 24 September beta notes corrected a number of quest rewards to the right item level and adjusted "a small number of early caster rewards"; wands no longer gain damage from the wielder's spell damage; Tame Beast no longer works on beasts above the Hunter's level. [wfguides-start]
- Hunter pets gain definition through gear scaling. [blizzard]
- New items, rewards and tradeskill content; account-wide Collections with new transmog, toys, mounts and companions; SD/HD character model toggle. [wiki]
- Library books (formerly mage-only) now for all classes: turn in 10, 20 and 25 books to Garion Wendell (Wizard's Sanctum, Stormwind City) or Owen Thadd (Magic Quarter, Undercity) for [20] Friend of the Library (Scholarly Pendant or Erudite's Amulet), [35] Greater Friend of the Library (Field Researcher's Loop or Philanthropist's Ring), [45] Greater Friend of the Library (Truthseeker's Bow, Crest of Elucidation, or Researcher's Nightlight). Mages who turn in at least one book learn [Study]. The SoD Comprehension Charm is now the mage [Comprehend Scroll] ability, which converts dropped scrolls (e.g. [Scroll: LOWER PING WHOMEVER]) into stat scrolls such as [Scroll of Intellect]; higher-skill scrolls yield cantrips like [Scroll of Liminal Passage] or [Scroll of the Blade] (turns a [Hypnotic Blade] into a [Narpas Sword]). [wiki]

### Rulesets and Hardcore
- You choose a Ruleset, not a realm: Normal, PvP, Roleplay-PvP, Roleplay; Hardcore planned to follow "a little after launch". Grouping, guilds, dungeons and raids only work within the same Ruleset and faction. On PvP realms each player is restricted to one faction. [blizzard]
- Players are split by ruleset (Normal/PvP/RP/RP-PvP/Hardcore) onto one "mega-realm" per ruleset per region; Hardcore comes later; characters have first and last names. [wiki]
- Hardcore timing: "a little after launch" [blizzard] vs "Winter 2026/2027" on the roadmap [wiki].

### Content phases / roadmap
- 17 September beta (until 17:00 UTC); 21 October stress test (21:00–25:00 UTC); 27 October early name reservation from 20:00 UTC, up to three reserved characters per account; 4/5 November launch at 23:00 UTC; 9 December raids unlock (Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit, Onyxia's Lair); Winter 2026/2027 Hardcore; Spring 2027: 2 new raids, 2 new dungeons, new quests and playable area, new legendary questline, PvP season refresh; Summer 2027: revamped iconic raid, new raid, expanded world content, 2 new dungeons, PvP season refresh, professions and Legacy updates. [wiki]
- Mount Hyjal's raids open 9 December 2026. [wfl-mounts] Site header: "Raids Dec 9, 2026". [wfguides-start]
- Launch 4 November 2026, 23:00 UTC (3:00 p.m. PST). [wfguides-start, timesaver-mount]
- Beta level cap was 20 at opening, raised to 30 with the 1 October update. [wfguides-start] Public beta stopped at level 30. [wfl-horde, wfl-mounts]
- End-of-beta event 22 October, 10 AM PDT (Kazzak destroying Brill, BoE Molten Core loot, Endless elixirs); event-only, not live content. [wiki]

### Quality of life
- Experience Presets: Classic and Enhanced, affecting presentation only, not Ruleset. Enhanced: quest guidance (points of interest, marked objective areas), one-bag view, transmog, strafe keybinds and centralized layout, HD models. Classic: SD models, classic layout, camera-turn keybinds, with quest guidance, one-bag view and transmog off by default. All individually adjustable in Game Menu > Options. Advanced options include a Cooldown Manager and a Damage Meter. Gamepad support exists in a beta state (select targets, cast, loot, menus); keyboard still needed for chat. [blizzard]
- Emphasis on community building over auto-matchmaking. [wiki]
- Barbershops: new ones in Darnassus and Thunder Bluff; all barbershops now staffed; city guards give directions to one. Creating or changing a party could transfer players to a different world instance (known issue); 1 October notes say party leaders should no longer be transferred, without claiming a full fix. [wfguides-start]
- Not covered by any of the seven pages: hearthstone changes, meeting stones, a dungeon finder (wfguides-start lists a "Group Finder" systems page in its navigation only), flight-path pricing, bag-size changes beyond the one-bag view and the 12-slot Sturdy Lunchbox reward.

### Crossing continents (Horde)

### Economy context and preparation advice
- Fresh economy: everyone starts at level 1 on launch day, no main to fund alts, empty auction house; no gold or character transfer announced from Classic Era, Season of Discovery or Anniversary; no WoW Token announced (Blizzard only promised "other ways to advance and grow at level 60"). [timesaver-mount] No gold transfers; start saving early. [wfl-mounts]

## 6. Caveats

### Page dates and freshness
- blizzard: undated in the captured text; launch-era content, but it never states gold prices, dungeon levels or zone ranges.
- wiki: edited 8 November 2026 (four days post-launch) — the freshest page; still carries beta-era material (end-of-beta event) and HTML-stripped numbers (dual spec "for 50", fee decays "to 1" — currency icons lost).
- wfl-horde: published 4 November, updated 7 November; explicitly "checked 4 November 2026"; the zone order above 30 is untested ("a plan, not a promise").
- wfguides-start: published 29 September, updated 3 November; most content is beta-client datamining (builds 1.60.1.70009 and 1.60.1.70058) and September/October beta notes, i.e. pre-launch.
- method-mounts: 21 October 2026, written during the level-30-capped beta; nothing at 40 or 60 was tested in-game.
- timesaver-mount: 4 November 2026 at 03:30 UTC, about 20 hours before servers opened; promises to re-check live trainers but the captured text predates that.
- wfl-mounts: 4 November 2026, launch day; prices described as beta-client values.

### Beta-era numbers vs launch
- All riding prices (100 / 20 / 1,000 / 200 gold) and the 10% Honored discount come from the beta client via Warcraft Tavern, Method and forum posts; Blizzard confirmed only the model (mount included with training), not the gold figures. [timesaver-mount, wfl-mounts]
- Trainer coordinates are labeled "(beta)". [wfl-mounts]
- Dungeon XP changes (1 and 8 October), respawn tuning, campfire damage rules, wand and Tame Beast changes, Poor Copper Vein reductions, and "Snowbound" XP are beta development notes for specific builds and may differ live. [wfl-horde, wfguides-start]

- The RestedXP new-quest counts are a 9 October snapshot; Blizzard's "over 1,000" is the full-game figure. [wfl-horde]
